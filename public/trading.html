Grading Engine

Every closed trade builds a card. The engine learns from those cards and produces two numbers per setup: an expectancy (drives the setup's base position size and, at the extreme, a kill switch) and — at each live signal — a quality grade A+/A/B/C from which of the additional checks are aligned right now.

Broker Profiles

Signals fan out to every enabled profile in Live mode. Paper profiles record locally and never send. Alpaca profiles hold their own key/secret/URL so you can have multiple accounts (paper + live) side by side; live submission is still stubbed pending your go-ahead.

Execution Mode

Off: signals fire but no order/position is created and nothing is sent anywhere — good for pure testing.
Paper: local record + fan-out to Alpaca profiles pointing at paper-api. Real fills, no real money.
Live: local record + real orders to Alpaca profiles pointing at the live URL. Requires the confirmation flag below.

Data Source

Polling fetches 1-min bars from Alpaca every 60 seconds (simpler; up to 60s lag on signal fire).
WebSocket streams live bars and quotes (real-time entry price; Alpaca creds required).
Both use the market-data feed (IEX / SIP) configured on the active Alpaca broker profile in the Brokers tab.
