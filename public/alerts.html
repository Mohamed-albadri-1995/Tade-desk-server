⚠ Alerts

← all tools
Two kinds of alert arrive here: rules every five minutes, setups once at a fixed minute.
why that matters
Rules are evaluated inside every screener on its own scan, so they need no separate data feed — and see the market roughly every five minutes. A break that happens and fails inside one interval is never seen.
Setups wake once at a fixed time, pull 1-minute bars for the whole card list and rank it. They are not watching all day; they answer one question at one moment.
1 · Standard account
A reference only — no order is ever placed against it. Broker accounts below are sized as a multiple of it.
Shares are worked out from what you lose if the stop is hit, not from a fixed dollar amount — risk per share runs from 0.3% to 5% of price across these candidates, so a fixed amount would risk fifteen times more on one than another. Leave these blank and alerts arrive without a share count.
1b · Calculatorentry · stop · target → shares
Worked out against the standard account above. Pressing Send it… asks which broker account to send to and re-prices it for that one — the share count is not the same in a half-size account.

3 · This machine applies to every account
With this ON, a setup that fires opens a real position by itself — entry, stop and target — with no tap from you. It needs two things to be true: this switch, and orders on the setup itself. Either one off means alert only, and a setup also has to name which account it sends to. Quantity is always whole shares and is reduced to fit the buying power left rather than sent to be rejected. Each account's buying power is a number you enter — SignalStack cannot be asked for it, so this side keeps its own tally per account and the broker's own refusal is the real backstop.
