Two different things arrive here, and they do not have the same resolution.
Rules are evaluated inside every screener on its own scan, so they need no separate data feed — and see the market roughly every five minutes. A break that happens and fails inside one interval is never seen.
Setups wake once at a fixed time, pull 1-minute bars for the whole card list and rank it. They are not watching all day; they answer one question at one moment.
Position size
Shares are worked out from what you lose if the stop is hit, not from a fixed dollar amount — risk per share runs from 0.3% to 5% of price across these candidates, so a fixed amount would risk fifteen times more on one than another. Leave these blank and alerts arrive without a share count.
Calculatorentry · stop · target → shares
Uses the account size and risk per trade above. Leave the target blank and it shows where 2R would be.
Algo trading off
With this ON, a setup that fires opens a real position by itself — entry, stop and target — with no tap from you. It needs two things to be true: this switch, and orders on the setup itself. Either one off means alert only. Quantity is always whole shares and is reduced to fit the buying power left rather than sent to be rejected. The buying power above is a number you enter — SignalStack cannot be asked for it, so this side keeps its own tally and the broker's own refusal is the real backstop.
